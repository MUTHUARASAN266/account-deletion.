Delete Your Arokya Unavu Account

Arokya Unavu allows you to request deletion of your account and the personal data associated with your account at any time.

Account deletion is currently not available directly within the app. To request account deletion, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your Arokya Unavu account to km.arasankpk266@gmail.com .
  2. Use the following subject line: Arokya Unavu — Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the name used to create your Arokya Unavu account so that we can identify your account.

We may request additional information when necessary to verify account ownership before processing the deletion request.

We will process your account deletion request within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your Arokya Unavu account.
  • Your name and email address associated with the account.
  • Your authentication credentials stored through Firebase Authentication.

What Is Not Affected

Arokya Unavu's food, recipe, remedy, and health-related content is shared app-wide and is not associated with an individual user account. Therefore, deleting your account does not affect this content.

We do not currently store additional personal data such as saved favorites on our servers. Any locally stored information on your device is removed when the app's local data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Data Retention

Retention: After your account deletion request is completed, the personal data associated with your account will be permanently deleted from our active systems within 30 days and cannot be recovered.
